Mission Analog Readiness

Team Mid-Sem Make Up, Delhi. Earth sites scored against the Shackleton–de Gerlache connecting ridge, with Arcadia Planitia (Badger) as the locked ground-ice case on Mars.

A similarity score is a partial match on the measured features, not a probability of mission success. A lower score is a closer partial match. The published scores used ndvi_median, precip_median_mm_hr, and lst_night_median_k. slope_median_deg was null in those scores. Gravity, vacuum or thin air, and radiation are stated below as limits. They are not rows in results/sources.csv.

The lunar slope rows are the locked Site01 tile: interquartile range 8.0-16.7 degrees, median 12.3 degrees, patch longitude -137.4771, patch latitude -89.4632, patch size 16 km (LOLA 5 m/pixel slope, Site01 connecting ridge).

Decision in brief

Among the two compared patches, Vinchina is the stronger candidate for a terrain-only mobility study: its three-proxy score is lower (0.167981 vs. 0.186845), and its later-measured median slope (9.48 degrees) lies inside the lunar 8.0-16.7 degree IQR. Kunlun's 7.08-degree median is below the band. Keep Kunlun as a contrasting lower-slope case; do not describe either site as a validated analog.

The score uses only June 2024 NDVI, precipitation, and night surface temperature. None has a direct Moon or Mars referent in the cited source table, and global slope was null, so the slope comparison is separate. This does not turn the proxy score into a mission suitability or success measure. The proposed paired rover trial has not been run; access, safety, and logistics have not been assessed.

Breaks

Slope misses the lunar band. Median slope is 7.08 degrees (Copernicus DEM, GLO-30 Public, 2021 release). The locked lunar interquartile range is 8.0-16.7 degrees, and the lunar median is 12.3 degrees. The Earth median lies outside that band. This slope was measured after the score and was not part of 0.186845.

Gravity is a limit, not a value computed from a raster. Gravity on the Moon is about one-sixth of Earth. Gravity on Mars is about three-eighths of Earth. This patch is a full-Earth-gravity outdoor test.

Atmosphere is a limit, not a value computed from a raster. The Moon is a vacuum. Mars has thin carbon-dioxide air, roughly one percent of Earth’s surface pressure. This patch has Earth’s air.

Radiation is a limit, not a value computed from a raster. Air and the magnetic field block most of it at Earth’s surface. An outdoor test here does not qualify electronics or crews for the surface dose on the Moon or Mars.

Night temperature stands alone at 258.8 kelvin. A permanently shadowed lunar floor is not a thermal analog for any outdoor Earth site. No Diviner temperature is in results/sources.csv.

No ice or permafrost row is in results/sources.csv. Ridgeline snow in the Worldview class is a surface class. It is not a measured ice body, so this patch is not an operations analog for sampling lunar ice.

How a mission would use it

Use Kunlun as the lower-slope site in a proposed comparison with Vinchina. After route-scale terrain, surface-material, access, and safety checks, run matched-length traverses with the same rover setup, payload, and speed. Record local slope and material; log wheel energy per distance, slip, and interruptions over repeated runs. Differences would be site-specific, not a pure slope effect. This is a proposal, not a completed field trial. Vacuum, radiation, and suit or landing-engine tests stay in a lab.

Confidence

Low. The published score omitted slope, and the later-measured median falls below the lunar band. No Earth ice or permafrost layer was used, so this site is not an ice-sampling analog.

The credibility pack is notes/credibility.md, notes/factor-coverage.md, and notes/sensitivity.md.

Mars

The case locked in notes/targets.md is ground ice. The region is Arcadia Planitia, the terraced crater Badger. No second Mars region was added. Bramson et al. (2015) used CTX imagery and SHARAD radargrams, checked against MOLA-based clutter simulations, and inferred a decameters-thick layer that is mostly excess water ice at a flat terrace 43 m deep (DOI). This is published target evidence; this project did not retrieve or process a Badger ice raster.

The Earth-side cold and vegetation proxies are 258.8 kelvin and NDVI -0.01995 at Kunlun, and 254.45 kelvin and NDVI 0.00925 at Vinchina. No Mars temperature or vegetation value is in results/sources.csv, so these Earth values stand alone. No Earth ice or permafrost layer was used; therefore, no Mars Earth analog is claimed and the Earth ice comparison remains open.
